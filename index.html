League Rules
2026/2027 Season
💰 Buy-In & Payouts
  • $100 buy-in per team, due before the first game on Friday, August 21.
  • Weekly and season-long payout structure will be finalized once league size is confirmed.
🤑 Prerequisites
  • Buy-in must be received before the first game kicks off on Friday, August 21.
  • If you were not part of the 2025/26 league, you must complete an intro Guinness shotgun on Saturday, August 22. Failure to do so results in immediate exclusion — buy-in will be returned.
🍺🔫 Guinness Shotgun Rules
  • Shotguns are assigned to the lowest-scoring team(s) each gameweek — typically 5–8% of the league.
  • Must use a standard 14.9 oz (440ml) or larger Guinness can.
  • Video must be sent between 6:00–7:30 AM in your local timezone, on the assigned Saturday (typically the first Saturday of the following gameweek).
🍺❌ Missing a Shotgun
  • Extreme circumstances? Just reach out ahead of time — we can easily arrange an alternative with no repercussions. 😊
  • Missing without prior arrangement makes you temporarily ineligible for all weekly and season-long payouts.
  • To restore eligibility, you must add an additional shotgun in your next eligible window. You remain ineligible until your tally reaches zero.
  • Hard cap of 3 shotguns owed at once. Reaching 4+ due to consecutive misses results in immediate removal from the league with no reimbursement.
👥 Co-Manager Rules
  1. Co-managers share one buy-in and one payout — only one shotgun video is required per team per week.
  2. Maximum of 2 co-managers per team.
  3. Co-managers must be declared before the first game of the season.
  4. Any new co-manager must complete the intro shotgun on the first Saturday of the season. If both co-managers are new, this is the only time both are required to do it.
