🥇 1ST PLACE — Gabriel Costa — Bench Boost Mercado
Los Miracúlos fired up the Bench Boost this week and let every single one of their 15 players contribute to a 106-point haul that nobody else could touch. Haaland did his thing — goal, assist, 2 bonus — and the doubled armband points got boosted even further by a backline that showed up hard, with Guéhi and Gabriel the defender combining for 26 points between them. The Bench Boost squeezed every drop out of a juicy gameweek, and Gabriel climbs four spots in the overall table to rank 28 while pocketing $30. Four top-4 finishes this season, three bottom-2 finishes — the man is not here to be boring.

🥈 2ND PLACE — Harrison Wang — Triple Haaland Pays The Bills
Six bottom-2 finishes on the season and Harrison Wang is out here cashing a $15 check — the range on this man is genuinely impressive. The Triple Captain chip on Haaland was the right call at the right time: a goal, an assist, and 2 bonus translated to 33 armband points, the highest captain return in the league this week. Tarkowski added a goal, an assist, and 3 bonus for 15 points to round out a brilliant supporting cast. Flying Tacos still hasn’t moved off rank 35 overall, but this week? Second place, baby. They’ll take it.

🥉 3RD PLACE — Robert Shellow — Eight Times, Like Clockwork
Eight top-4 finishes for Robert Shellow this season. Eight. The Ahsenal tripled up on Haaland with the Triple Captain chip and the Norwegian delivered — goal, assist, 2 bonus, 33 points from the armband — while Semenyo chipped in a goal for 12. Robert is parked at rank 20 overall and hasn’t moved all season, which feels like a massive understatement of how consistently he’s been finishing in the money. Third place, $10, barely breaking a sweat.

4️⃣ 4TH PLACE — Luke Civantos & Liam Kelly — Two Winners, One Prize
Luke Civantos and Liam Kelly both Triple Captained Haaland, both got 33 points from the armband, both scored 93 — and somehow had to split fourth place like two kids fighting over the last slice of pizza and both ending up with half. Luke is quietly ascending the overall table to rank 7, one of the cleanest trajectories in the league all season. Liam’s Hot Chicken Albion is right behind at rank 11, climbing two spots. Two and a half dollars each. Champions.

🍺 SHOTGUN DUTY — Pedro, Bowen, And The Bottom Of The Barrel
Rob DiSanto has seven top-4 finishes on the season — more than almost anyone in this league — and yet here he is at 45 points, on shotgun duty for the fourth time, having captained João Pedro to what appears to have been a historically quiet afternoon. Guéhi and Haaland kept the lights on but the captain was a disaster, and Bangers and Cash slide four spots down the overall table to rank 26. The highs and lows of Rob DiSanto could fill a Netflix documentary.
Then there’s Richard Katz — 20 points. This is not a typo. Richard captained Jarrod Bowen for 4 points, deployed B. Fernandes for 3, and somehow started Hermansen in goal for 2. His three best players combined for 9 points. His entire team scored 20. In a week where the average was somewhere around 80. This is Richard’s fourth shotgun duty of the season, and at this point the Saturday morning Guinness isn’t a punishment — it’s just part of his routine. Both of you: 7:30am, cans cold, no negotiations.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
