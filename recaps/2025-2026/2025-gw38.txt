GW38: THE FINAL WHISTLE HAS BLOWN

📋 GW38 HEADLINES

🥇 Thomas Mayer caps a perfect season: zero bottom-2 finishes, $30 richer, and Bruno delivered
🥈 Richard Katz wins $15 he legally cannot touch until he pays his three-Guinness tab
🥉 Triple Captain Bowen hits for Mitch Maisel — landing tied third on the final day
🥉 Tucker Cina sneaks into the split third with Watkins doing the heavy lifting
🍺 John Morcos captains Gabriel. Jack Bernatchez Triple Captains Virgil. Both men must shotgun at dawn.

🥇 1ST PLACE — Thomas Mayer — Zero Guinnesses, All Glory

Six top-4 finishes, zero bottom-2 finishes, and a GW38 win to close the book — Thomas Mayer’s season reads like a bedtime story written for children who grow up to be insufferable. He handed Bruno the armband and watched him chip in a goal, an assist, a clean sheet, and three bonus points for a monstrous 28-point captain haul. Add in Bowen and Porro doing their parts, and Maximus FC finishes on 2119 — a triumphant march into the final weekend. Thirty dollars, no regrets, and frankly a little disgusting.

🥈 2ND PLACE — Richard Katz — $15 And A Three-Shotgun Deficit

Richard Katz put together a genuinely excellent 76-point week — Bowen under the armband paid off handsomely, Hermansen kept a clean sheet, and the squad held together nicely. The problem is that Richard has been ducking his GW36 shotgun obligation for two consecutive Saturdays, and each missed week adds another can to the pile. He now owes three Guinnesses, and the $15 prize sits untouched until every last one is accounted for. He won second place and has the most outstanding bar tab in league history. Remarkable discipline in all the wrong areas.

🥉 3RD PLACE — Mitch Maisel — Triple Cap, Triple The Chaos

Mitch saw Bowen and said: give him all the points. He deployed the Triple Captain chip, transferred in Bowen, and watched him score, assist, keep a clean sheet, and bank three bonus — turning a 12-point return into 36. That one decision was the entire week. The rest of the squad was fairly pedestrian, and Haaland blanking on the bench was peak end-of-season comedy, but it didn’t matter. The Triple Cap gamble paid off and Mitch walks away with $7.50 and absolutely no need to apologize for anything.

🥉 3RD PLACE — Tucker Cina — Watkins Carried The Bag

Tucker matches Mitch on 70 points and splits the third-place prize fair and square. Bowen and Bruno did their jobs, but the real story was Ollie Watkins coming up clutch with a brace and three bonus for 13 points — quietly one of the best individual returns of the week. Tucker climbed eight spots in the overall standings on the final day, which is the kind of momentum that means absolutely nothing now that the season is over, but still feels great.

🍺 SHOTGUN DUTY — Captaining Defenders Was Always Going To End Like This

Two managers. Two catastrophic captain decisions. One Saturday morning ritual.

John Morcos finishes on 25 points after captaining Gabriel, who returned a 1-pointer. His entire squad was a wasteland — Haaland, Saka, Doku, and João Pedro all blanked, and his bench contributed precisely nothing. He ended the season ranked seventh overall, a genuinely strong full-season performance completely undermined by one catastrophic armband choice on the final day. A fitting send-off for a man who had it all and gave the armband to a centre-back.

Jack Bernatchez did something even more spectacular: he used his Triple Captain chip — his last chip of the season, the nuclear option — and put it on Virgil van Dijk, who returned 3 points. The 3x multiplier transformed a 3-pointer into a 6-pointer in what may be the most expensive miscalculation in Fantasi Futbol history. Six bottom-2 finishes on the season, and he saved his most creative one for last. Both of you: 7:30am Saturday, cans ready, no exceptions.
