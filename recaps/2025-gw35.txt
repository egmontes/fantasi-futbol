🥇 1ST PLACE — Jack Bernatchez — GYÖKERES CARRIED HIM HOME
Forget Haaland for a second — it was Viktor Gyökeres who turned Feed Your Horses into a weekly highlight reel, going off for two goals, an assist, and three bonus points in a 16-point masterclass that carried Jack’s team to 84 on the week. Haaland did his solid-if-unspectacular thing on the armband — one goal, one bonus, a return that earns its points through the doubling rather than any kind of explosion — and Senesi chipped in another 14 from the back like the quietly absurd defender he’s become. Jack has now top-foured three times this season despite five bottom-2 finishes, which means he lives entirely in extremes, and this week the chaos landed exactly right. $30 well and truly earned.

🥈 2ND PLACE — John Heyer — BENCH BOOST, ALMOST PERFECT
John Heyer activated the Bench Boost and turned in 81 points, which in any other week wins the gameweek outright — unfortunately he ran directly into Jack Bernatchez and his pet Swedish striker. Haaland delivered on the armband, Okafor added a goal and a clean sheet for a tidy 10, and every single player in the squad contributed, which is the whole point of the chip. He’s climbing the overall table and collecting $15 in the process — the only thing keeping this from being a perfect week is the three points standing between him and the big check.

🥉 3RD PLACE — Jimmy Romanelli — CONSISTENT. BORING. RICH.
Six top-4 finishes, zero bottom-2 finishes, fourth in the overall table, and $10 added to the pile — Los Porro Hermanos is running a business while the rest of this league is running a feelings journal. The Bench Boost brought in a solid 77, Haaland did his job on the armband, and Okafor and Kelleher both contributed cleanly from the support cast. There’s nothing chaotic to roast here, which is honestly the most unsettling thing about Jimmy.

4️⃣ 4TH PLACE — Tommy Harris — TWENTY BENCH POINTS, STILL RICH
Tommy Harris left 20 points rotting on his bench this week and still scraped into the money, which is the kind of outcome that should come with a formal apology letter to every manager who finished fifth. Senesi delivered an absurd 14 from the back, Haaland matched him on the armband, and Hill added 10 more — the starting 11 did enough even while a small fortune sat unused. Six top-4 finishes on the season means Tommy knows how to show up when it counts, even if his bench management is genuinely concerning.

🍺 SHOTGUN DUTY — TWO CAPTAINS WALK INTO A BAR
Tyler Wasser captained Florian Wirtz. Tucker Cina captained Ollie Watkins. On a week when the armband answer was more obvious than a penalty shootout villain, these two looked at Haaland, shrugged, and went rogue — finishing 36th and 37th respectively with 40 and 39 points. Tyler is now on five bottom-2 finishes for the season, a number that should come with its own loyalty card, and Tucker just added a fourth stamp to his. The best players either of them had all week were their goalkeepers, which really says everything. 7:30am Saturday, gentlemen. The Guinness doesn’t care about your captaincy logic.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
