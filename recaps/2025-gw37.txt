🥇 1ST PLACE — Michael Legaz — WATKINS DOES THE HEAVY LIFTING

When Ollie Watkins bags two goals, an assist, and max bonus points, being the guy who captained him feels less like genius and more like divine intervention — but Legaz will take it either way, thank you very much. The 30-point captain haul was the backbone of an 84-point week that also featured Rogers and Cunha chipping in double digits. Yes, Richarlison scored 7 on the bench, and yes, Van de Ven was a mannequin. But $30 richer and climbing the weekly table, Legaz is officially the commissioner’s favorite person this week.

🥈 2ND PLACE — Eric Wallerstein — CONSISTENT ERIC DOESN’T MISS

Six top-4 finishes. Zero shotgun duties. An 82-point week anchored by Haaland, Watkins, and a Virgil van Dijk who apparently remembered he can score goals. Wallerstein is the most quietly terrifying manager in this league — no drama, no chaos, just perpetually cashing checks. The B.Fernandes-to-Saka swap landed cleanly. This is what competence looks like, and it’s honestly a little annoying.

🥉 3RD PLACE — Tucker Cina — BENCH HOARDING HALL OF SHAME

Tucker gets the podium, and Tucker deserves a firm handshake immediately followed by a gentle slap, because Anderson scored 12 points on his bench. Twelve. The Bruno captain came good with a doubled 18, Watkins and Virgil did their thing, but 14 bench points left rotting is the kind of waste that haunts a man. Still, 79 points and $10 in pocket — worst problems to have heading into the final week.

4️⃣ 4TH PLACE — Tyler Wasser & James McMullen — HALF A FIVER EACH, SPLIT THE SHAME

A wildcard from Wasser that delivered 78 points — respectable, though Rodon’s 6 points warming the bench suggests the rebuild wasn’t quite perfect. McMullen matched him with a genuinely inspired Calvert-Lewin captain (14 doubled points, clean sheet, bonus) and Darlow pulling 11 out of thin air at GK. Two very different routes to the exact same destination, sharing $2.50 each like two blokes splitting a bag of crisps. The season’s fifth top-4 for McMullen; Wasser gets his fourth. At least they’re not drinking Saturday morning.

🍺 SHOTGUN DUTY — THE GABRIEL CAPTAIN SUPPORT GROUP

Jimmy Romanelli and Mitchel Pattullo both looked at the Arsenal fixture, decided Gabriel was the move, doubled him up — and collected a whopping 12 points on the armband while Watkins was out there playing the video game on easy mode. Jimmy’s Henderson at GK went full ghost (0 pts), and nearly his entire bench scored nothing. Mitchel left Rogers (12 pts) and Bornauw (6) on the bench while fielding Mateta and Cherki — bold. Ryan Kelly said “hold my beer” and captained Beto, which returned 4 points, which is genuinely impressive in the worst possible way. And Harrison Wang, bless him, captained Guéhi — a defender — for 4 points, in a week where Watkins scored 15 uncaptained. Seven bottom-2 finishes on the season for Harrison; this league is his personal Groundhog Day. Four managers, four Guinnesses, 7:30am Saturday. The bar doesn’t care about your captain logic.
